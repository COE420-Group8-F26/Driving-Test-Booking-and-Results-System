# Stakeholders

## Stakeholder Analysis

This document identifies the key stakeholders involved in the Driving Test Booking System, their roles, interests/expectations, level of influence on the system, and how the system affects each of them.

| Stakeholder | Role | Interest/Expectations | Influence on the System | How the System Affects the Stakeholder |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Applicants** | Individuals who register and book driving tests using the system. | - Easy registration and booking<br>- Accurate slot availability<br>- Simple cancellation or rescheduling<br>- On-time notifications<br>- Easy access to test history and results | **High** — Applicant needs and feedback directly affect system functionality and usability. | Allows applicants to manage and book driving tests, get updates, and access test results and histories without the need for manual processes. |
| **Driving Examiners** | Conduct the driving tests and record results of applicants. | - Access to accurate schedules, assigned tests, and applicant information<br>- Efficient method to submit pass/fail results | **High** — Driving examiner feedback and workflow influence scheduling, examiner assignment, and result management functionalities. | Allows examiners to digitally submit test results and have access to organized schedules and assigned tests. |
| **Administrators** | Manage test bookings, records, schedules, and examiner assignments. | - Efficient management<br>- Accurate record access<br>- Control over schedules<br>- Reliable access to test and booking information | **Very High** — Administrators are responsible for major system operations, and their requirements have a great impact on system functionality. | The system centralizes admin tasks, creating ease in managing schedules, bookings, test records, and examiners. |
| **Driving Test Authority** | Regulates and oversees the driving-test process. | - Access to accurate test records and reliable results<br>- Standardized procedures<br>- Organized testing process | **Very High** — Organizational policies and regulations can determine how the system deals with records, tests, and results. | Provides a transparent and organized process for maintaining records and managing driving tests. |
| **IT** | Provide, maintain, and monitor system technical support. | - System security, availability, reliability, maintainability, and appropriate handling of technical issues | **Medium** — Maintenance and technical requirements affect system security, reliability, and long-run success. | Reduces the need for manual maintenance and allows efficient monitoring and maintenance through a centralized system. |

## Summary

The stakeholder analysis highlights that **Administrators** and the **Driving Test Authority** hold the highest influence over the system, as they directly shape operational and regulatory requirements. **Applicants** and **Driving Examiners** also exert high influence through their day-to-day usage and workflow needs, ensuring the system remains user-centered and functionally accurate. **IT** plays a supporting but essential role in maintaining system reliability, security, and long-term stability.
